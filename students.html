Information for Prospective Students

If you are a student at NYU Shanghai, I am happy to meet with you in person to chat about research opportunities, grad school, career options, classes, etc. For everyone else, feel free to email me and I will do my best to respond (time permitting).

First contact

Please include a brief introduction of yourself and why you’re interested in working with me. Also attach your CV/resume and transcript.

Do not send me some long-winded, LLM generated, email. I will ignore these emails out of principle.

What I’m looking for

I am interested in working with students who demonstrate:

  • strong writing and communication skills
  • a commitment to fostering community
  • ability to integrate and sythensize complex ideas

PhD students

NYU Shanghai is a unique and exciting environment for PhD studies.

  • Your first first year will be in NYC (for courses). In subsequent years, you’ll be based primarily in Shanghai.
  • Upon graduation, you will recieve a degree from NYU You can read more here.

Prospective PhD students should apply to the NYU CS PhD program (this is the same application as for Courant/Tandon). Indicate your interest in NYU Shanghai.

Undergraduate students

I ocassionally have funded research opportunties for undergrads. To be considered, you should have done well in upper-level computer science and math courses. The availability of these positions is constrained by my capacity not by funding.
